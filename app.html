Henry Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out inductance, currents, flux and stored energy from the numbers you type. The inductances and currents are yours to measure or read off your own circuit. It treats every inductor as ideal, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the core, saturation, resistance, heat or insulation, and it gives no verdict that an inductor, a winding or a supply is adequate or safe. A charged inductor can bite when its circuit opens; every number here is your own entry.

The energy truth

Flux linkage and stored energy for one inductor. Inductance in µH, current in A.

The series truth

Two inductors in series add up, and the stored energy splits in proportion.

The parallel truth

Two inductors in parallel shrink, and the current splits in inverse proportion.
