Henry Math

A 100 µH inductor carrying 2 A links 200 µWb of flux and stores 0.2 mJ. Two in parallel act like 31.97 µH and split the current in inverse proportion.

Boundary: arithmetic only Henry Math works out inductance, currents, flux and stored energy from the numbers you type. The inductances and currents are yours to measure or read off your own circuit. It treats every inductor as ideal, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the core, saturation, resistance, heat or insulation, and it gives no verdict that an inductor, a winding or a supply is adequate or safe. A charged inductor can bite when its circuit opens; every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 100 µH at 2 A, and a 100 µH with a 47 µH in series and in parallel.

The energy truth

A 100 µH inductor carrying 2 A links 200 µWb of flux and stores 0.2 mJ of energy.

The series truth

100 µH and 47 µH in series act like 147 µH. At 2 A the pair stores 0.29 mJ, with 0.2 mJ in the 100 µH one and 0.09 mJ in the 47 µH one.

The parallel truth

100 µH and 47 µH in parallel act like 31.97 µH. A 2 A total splits into 0.64 A in the 100 µH one and 1.36 A in the 47 µH one.
